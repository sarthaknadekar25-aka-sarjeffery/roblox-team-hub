04 · Good to know

Questions, answered

✦
Is this paid work?

We start most roles on rev-share with clear milestones. Exceptional contributors move to paid per-task or monthly stipends once the game monetises. Choose “Open to discuss” if flexible.

I'm under 16 — can I still apply?

Yes for Tester, Builder trainee, Animator and Composer. Manager requires 16+ for moderation duties.

What happens after I apply?

Your app hits our Discord + admin dashboard instantly. We review within ~48h, then reach out on Discord for a short trial task or interview.

Do I need to be online every day?

No — just hit your weekly hours and deadlines. One 30-min sync per week is the only fixed meeting.

Who owns what I create?

Work made for Sarthak's Studio belongs to the project (covered by the NDA), but you always get public credit + portfolio rights.
